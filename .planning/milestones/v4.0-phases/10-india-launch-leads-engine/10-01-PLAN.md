   - Add verified ATS entries (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, SmartRecruiters, Workday) with accurate slugs and domain tags.

2. **Harden Regional Metro Hub Classifier (`backend/app/utils/classifier.py`)**:
   - Ensure detection for Bengaluru/Bangalore, Delhi NCR (Gurugram/Gurgaon, Noida), Hyderabad, Pune, Mumbai, Chennai, Kolkata, Ahmedabad, Kochi, and Remote-India.
