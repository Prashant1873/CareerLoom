# Plan 10-02: India-First Discovery Controls & CTC Compensation Badges

## Goal
Deliver an India-first discovery experience across the Obsidian 3-column control center adhering to `frontend-skill`: a 1-click "India Direct" mode in `NavRail.tsx`, a Metro Tech Hub filter ribbon (BLR, NCR, HYD, PUN, BOM, MAA), prominent INR CTC compensation badges on `JobCard.tsx` and `JobInspectorPane.tsx`, and NLP query parser expansion for Indian tech seniority levels and city synonyms.

## Requirements Covered
- **INDIA-DISCOVER-03**: Interface provides dedicated "India Direct" toggle in the navigation rail, Indian Metro Hub filter ribbon (BLR, NCR, HYD, PUN, BOM, MAA), and Indian role level NLP expansion (SDE-1/2/3, APM, EM).
- **INDIA-CTC-04**: Detect and display INR CTC compensation indicators (e.g. ₹20-35 LPA, ₹50 LPA+) on job cards and inspector pane.

   - Add a high-visibility "India Direct" quick-filter switch with flag icon and live Indian job count badge.
   - When toggled, instantly sets country filter to `India` and updates stream with zero latency.
2. **Metro Tech Hub Quick Filter Ribbon (`frontend/src/components/MasterJobStream.tsx` & `FilterSidebar.tsx`)**:
   - Add quick-select chips for top Indian tech hubs: All India, Bengaluru (BLR), NCR / Gurgaon / Noida, Hyderabad (HYD), Pune (PUN), Mumbai (BOM), Chennai (MAA), and Remote (India).

   - Parse and extract Indian compensation patterns from job title/description: e.g. `₹18-30 LPA`, `₹40L+`, `₹25-45 Lakhs`.

   - Add Indian engineering hierarchy synonyms: `SDE 1` / `SDE 2` / `SDE 3`, `APM` / `GPM`, `MTS` / `SMTS`, `EM` / `Director of Eng`.
   - Map Indian city abbreviations and informal names (e.g., `Bangalore`, `Gurgaon`, `Noida`, `Cyberabad`, `BLR`, `NCR`) directly to canonical tech hubs.
