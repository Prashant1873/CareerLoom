# Plan 08-01: Persistent Job Inspector & Embedded Community Intel

## Goal
Build the persistent right-side inspector pane (`JobInspectorPane.tsx`), eliminating disruptive modal dialogs by embedding the job overview, verified apply CTA, and tabbed Reddit community intelligence (turnaround estimates, stage roadmap, tips, discussions) directly into the 3-column control center.

## Context & Requirements
- **Requirements**: V2-INSPECT-01, V2-INSPECT-02, V2-AESTHETIC-01, V2-MOTION-01
- **Adheres to**: `frontend-skill` (persistent inspection pane, zero layout shifts, fluid tab switcher, Apple spring transitions).

## Tasks

### Task 1: Job Inspector Pane Component (`frontend/src/components/JobInspectorPane.tsx`)
- Sticky right-side panel:
  - Header: Large employer logo, verified ATS badge, company name, domain tag, role title.
  - Action Bar: Direct Apply on Official Portal button, Bookmark button with count, Share link.
  - Quick Info Matrix: Location, Work Mode (Remote/Hybrid), Date Posted with freshness tier badge.
  - Tabs Switcher:
    - **Tab 1: Overview**: Role summary, tag pills, requirement keywords.
    - **Tab 2: Reddit Intel**: Live turnaround metric (e.g. `~2-3 weeks`), stepped interview stages roadmap, candidate insider tips, and clickable Reddit thread cards.

### Task 2: Responsive Fallback & Mobile Sheet
- On desktop (>1200px): Persistent Column 3 in split layout.
- On tablet & mobile (<1200px): Slide-over drawer with backdrop overlay.

### Task 3: Final Integration, Styling & Verification
- Mount `JobInspectorPane` in Column 3 of `App.tsx`.
- Refine animations with Apple spring physics (`active: scale(0.98)`).
- Verify with `npm run build` and run full backend tests.
