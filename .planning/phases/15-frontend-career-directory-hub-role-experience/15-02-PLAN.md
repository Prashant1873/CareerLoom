<threat_model>
ASVS Level: 1
Threats:
- T-15-03 (LOW): Excessive Background Polling — Polling `/api/targets/sync/status` too frequently drains client CPU and creates unnecessary backend request volume.
  Mitigation: Poll sync status at 15-second intervals only when a sync is active (`is_running === true`), otherwise poll conservatively every 60 seconds or on modal mount.
- T-15-04 (LOW): Filter Desynchronization — Mismatch between UI ribbon state and actual `FilterState` query dispatched to `/api/jobs`.
  Mitigation: Bind quick ribbons directly to `filters.category === 'product_cluster'` and `filters.category === 'strategy_cluster'` with atomic toggle handlers.
</threat_model>
