# Plan 10-03: Candidate Lead Capture & Fast Referral Engine

## Goal
Implement a candidate lead capture and referral acceleration engine enabling Indian job seekers to submit structured profiles (LinkedIn, YoE, current/expected CTC in LPA, notice period, resume link) directly from job cards or the inspector pane, persist leads in SQLite, and generate 1-click high-conversion referral request cards for WhatsApp, LinkedIn, Slack, and Discord.

## Requirements Covered
- **INDIA-LEADS-05**: Interactive candidate lead capture drawer allowing Indian job seekers to submit leads (LinkedIn, YoE, target role, expected CTC, resume) with SQLite `candidate_leads` storage, REST API endpoints, and one-click formatted lead cards for referral communities.

## Tasks
1. **Backend Database Schema & Lead Model (`backend/app/models/lead.py` & `backend/app/db/database.py`)**:
   - Define `CandidateLead` Pydantic model with strict validation.
   - Add `candidate_leads` SQLite table with columns: `id`, `job_id`, `company`, `role_title`, `candidate_name`, `email`, `linkedin_url`, `yoe`, `current_ctc_lpa`, `expected_ctc_lpa`, `notice_period_days`, `resume_url`, `status`, `created_at`.
   - Add DB functions: `insert_lead()`, `get_leads()`, `get_leads_by_job()`.
2. **REST API Endpoints (`backend/app/api/leads.py` & `backend/app/main.py`)**:
   - `POST /api/leads/submit`: Validates and commits candidate leads with timestamp.
   - `GET /api/leads`: Query leads with optional filtering by company, status, or date.
   - Register route in FastAPI application.
3. **Frontend Lead Capture Modal (`frontend/src/components/LeadCaptureModal.tsx`)**:
   - Designed adhering to `frontend-skill` with Obsidian dark styling and Apple spring motion.
   - Inputs: Full Name, Email, LinkedIn URL, Years of Experience (YoE), Current CTC & Expected CTC (LPA), Notice Period dropdown (Immediate, 15 Days, 30 Days, 60 Days, 90 Days), and Resume Link.
   - Integrated CTA on `JobCard.tsx` ("Ask Referral") and `JobInspectorPane.tsx` ("Submit Candidate Lead / Referral").
4. **One-Click Referral Message Generator**:
   - Auto-generate polished, high-converting referral pitch customized to the candidate's details and selected job.
   - One-click "Copy Referral Pitch" button with tactile copy feedback for direct sharing with company employees on LinkedIn or alumni networks.
